---
description: Ongoing fee arrangements by consent window: lapsed, closing soon, opening soon.
---

Run `npm run advice -- consents-due --days=60`.

Present three groups:
1. **Lapsed.** No consent inside the window, so the arrangement has ended (Corporations Act s962F). Say plainly: stop the fee with the platform today, then `end-arrangement`. Never suggest back-dating a consent.
2. **Closing.** Open windows with no consent yet, by days left.
3. **Opening soon.** Windows that open in the next 30 days: the time to send the form.

Offer `/draft-consent` for anything in groups 2 and 3. NZ rows are practice policy, not the AU statute: say so.
